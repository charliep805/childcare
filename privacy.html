Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 27, 2026

At Paz Family Childcare, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to our website. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website and how it is used.

Information We Collect

This website is provided for informational purposes only. We do not collect personal information through this website. We do not offer online enrollment, contact forms, newsletter sign-ups, or other features that ask visitors to provide personal information.

We use Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics may automatically collect technical information, including:

  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of visits
  • Approximate geographic location
  • IP address and similar technical information

This information helps us understand website traffic and improve our website. We do not use this information to identify individual visitors.

Cookies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies through Google Analytics to analyze website traffic and improve website performance. You can control or disable cookies through your web browser settings.

How We Use Information

Information collected through Google Analytics is used only to:

  • Understand how visitors use our website.
  • Improve website content and performance.
  • Monitor general website traffic and usage trends.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information collected through this website.

Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google. Google may collect and process information according to its own privacy practices.

More information about Google's privacy practices can be found here: Google Privacy Policy .

You may opt out of Google Analytics by using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on .

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including social media pages and government websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

Children's Privacy

Although this website provides information about our family childcare program, it is intended for parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
